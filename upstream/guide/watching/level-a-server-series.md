## What it is for

The episodes of a series on your Jellyfin server play at one volume, so you stop reaching for the
volume between one episode and the next.

## How to use it

1. Open the menu of a series, a season or an episode on your server and choose **Level the volume…**.
2. Keep **All languages**, or click **Choose** and tick the audio languages to level.
3. For a series, turn on **Measure new episodes too** to level the episodes the server adds later.
4. Click **Level the volume**. Episodes are measured while nothing plays; a badge at the top counts them.
5. **Levelling details…** shows each episode's gain, or why it has none, and **Retry** for a failed one.

## Good to know

- **Restore original volume** stops levelling but keeps the measurements, so levelling again is instant.
- In the player, **Audio › Sound › Original volume** plays the title without its gain, to compare by ear.
- The level is **Target level for films and series** in **Settings › Audio**, even with **Same loudness for every title** off.
