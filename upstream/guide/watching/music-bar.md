## What it is for

Browse your library, or anything else in Kaveo, while a track plays, with its controls at the bottom
of the window.

![An album page with the music bar at the bottom: on the left the cover, the title, the artist and the album, and the next track; in the middle shuffle, previous, pause, next and repeat over the time and the position bar; on the right the volume, the queue and the stop button](../images/watching/music-bar.webp)

## How to use it

1. While a track plays, click the back button at the top left of the player: the track's album opens,
   and the music goes on.
2. Use the bar's buttons to pause, skip, shuffle the queue or repeat it; click or drag along the
   position bar under them to move in the track, and set the volume on the right.
3. Click the cover or the title to return to the player, the artist or the album to open its page.
4. Click the cross to stop.

## Good to know

- Repeat has three states: off, the whole list, and this track. The track's own end repeats it;
  Next still moves on.
- Music keeps its own shuffle and repeat, apart from videos', and Kaveo remembers them after a
  restart. With shuffle on, an album or playlist you play starts shuffled.
- After a restart, the bar shows *Queue from your last session*: the play button starts it again.
- The queue's **Save as playlist…** keeps it under a name: see [Playlists](../library/playlists.md).
