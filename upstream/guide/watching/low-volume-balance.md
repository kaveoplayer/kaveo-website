## What it is for

At a low volume the ear hears bass and treble less than voices. **Low-volume balance** lifts them as
you turn down, and leaves the sound alone at your normal volume.

![The Low-volume balance group in Settings › Audio › Effects: Lift bass and treble at a low volume on, the line At 70 %: bass +0.9 dB, treble +0.5 dB, Bass lift 6 dB, Treble lift 3 dB, Normal volume 100 % with the button to use the current volume, and Range 20 dB](../images/watching/low-volume-balance.webp)

## How to use it

1. In **Settings › Audio › Effects**, turn on **Lift bass and treble at a low volume**.
2. Play something at the volume you like, then press the button beside **Normal volume**.
3. **Bass lift** and **Treble lift** set the most that is added; **Range** sets how far below your normal volume it is all added.

## Good to know

- It does not act with **Volume control** on **Output device**, nor while a receiver sets the level and Kaveo stays at 100 %; the page says so.
- It belongs to the output, not to a preset, and turning **Effects** off turns it off too.
- The equalizer's **Bass and treble lift** preset stays the same at every volume; this one follows it.
