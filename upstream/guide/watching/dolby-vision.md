## What it is for

A Dolby Vision film carries, beside its HDR picture, instructions for every scene and sometimes a
second layer of detail. Kaveo applies both, so the film looks the way it was graded rather than as
plain HDR10.

## How to use it

1. Open a Dolby Vision film like any other: there is nothing to turn on.
2. On an HDR display, leave **HDR output** on **Auto — HDR when the display supports it** in **Settings › Display & HDR**.
3. On an SDR display, Kaveo fits the picture to it by itself.

## Good to know

- Profiles 5, 7, 8 and 10 play in full, a profile 7's enhancement layer included.
- A profile 7 whose enhancement layer is a separate video stream, as on a Blu-ray disc, still plays as HDR10.
- A profile 5 film shows no picture under the pointer on the seek bar: without its scene instructions the colours would be wrong.
