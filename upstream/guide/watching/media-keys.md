## What it is for

While a title plays, your desktop knows it: the keyboard's media keys play, pause and skip, and the
desktop's media controls — the media widget, the lock screen, a phone paired with the computer —
show its name and picture.

## How to use it

1. Start a film, an episode or a track.
2. Press the keyboard's play/pause key, or its next and previous keys, even with Kaveo's window in
   the background.
3. Or use the desktop's media widget: it shows the title, its poster and where you are, and you can
   drag its bar to move through the title.
4. To keep the title off the desktop, untick **System media controls** in
   [Settings › Interface](../settings/interface.md).

## Good to know

- Next and previous follow the same list as the player's own buttons; see
  [Next episode and queue](next-episode.md).
- Stop ends the title, as the player's back button does.
- A private title is shown like any other while it plays. Available on Linux for now.
