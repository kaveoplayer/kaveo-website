## What it is for

HDR10+ is an HDR format that tells the player, scene by scene, how bright the picture really is. A
dark scene is then not compressed as if it held the brightest highlight of the whole film, so it keeps
its detail. Kaveo reads this information by itself.

## How to use it

1. Open an HDR10+ film: there is nothing to turn on.
2. On an HDR display, leave **HDR output** on **Auto — HDR when the display supports it** in **Settings › Display & HDR**.
3. On an SDR display, Kaveo fits each scene to it by itself.

## Good to know

- Kaveo uses the scene information to fit the picture to your screen; it is not sent to the display.
- In the statistics overlay (**I**, then **Tab**), the **MEDIA** page shows HDR10+ when a film carries it.
- A film with both Dolby Vision and HDR10+ plays with its Dolby Vision information: see [Dolby Vision](dolby-vision.md).
- **Save HDR frame** keeps the picture as the film stores it, before any fitting.
