## What it is for

The equalizer raises or lowers the bass, the middle and the treble of everything you play, music and
video alike.

![The Equalizer card on Settings › Audio › Effects, open: the switch on, the Preset set to Bass and treble lift, Bass, Treble and Tilt at 0 dB, the Preamp at −2.5 dB, the Response curve, and Edit bands (10)](../images/watching/equalizer.webp)

## How to use it

1. In **Settings › Audio › Effects** the **Equalizer** is the first card: turn it on there.
2. Pick a **Preset**, such as **Bass boost** or **Vocal**, or open **Edit bands** and move them yourself.
3. **Preamp** leaves room when you raise several bands; **Response** draws what they do together.
4. **Save as…** keeps your own curve, in the same list under **Preset** everywhere.
5. While playing, right-click the picture, open **Audio › Sound › Effects**: the **Equalizer** is the
   first row of **Stages**, its tick switches it and its arrow opens the curves.

## Good to know

- Choosing a preset turns the equalizer on; **Off** in its menu keeps your bands for next time.
- It is part of the chain: with **Effects** off it is not heard, and **D** skips it with the rest.
- It does not act on Dolby or DTS sound sent undecoded to a receiver with **Bitstream passthrough**; use the receiver's own tone controls there.
- Changes are heard straight away and fade in smoothly, without a click.
