## What it is for

Watch the sound of a track while it plays: its spectrum, or its waveform.

![The player on a track: in the cover's place, bars in the accent colour rising from the low notes on the left to the high ones on the right, the track's title and artist under them, and the playback bar at the bottom with the visualizer button lit](../images/watching/visualizer.webp)

## How to use it

1. Play a track.
2. Click the visualizer button on the playback bar, beside the volume: the spectrum takes the cover's place.
3. Click it again for the oscilloscope, and once more to bring the cover back.
4. Kaveo remembers your choice. **Visualizer** in **Settings › Audio** sets it too.

## Good to know

- It shows what you hear, when you hear it, after the volume and the [equalizer](equalizer.md):
  lower the volume and the bars drop with it.
- A track sent undecoded to a receiver by **Bitstream passthrough** has nothing for Kaveo to draw,
  and the player says so.
- On a narrow window the lyrics take the cover's place, and the visualizer gives way to them.
