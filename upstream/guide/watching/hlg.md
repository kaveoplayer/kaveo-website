## What it is for

HLG is the HDR format of television broadcasts and of many phone cameras. Unlike an HDR film, it does
not say how bright its master was: the picture adapts to the screen it is shown on. Kaveo plays it
like any other video.

## How to use it

1. Open an HLG video: there is nothing to turn on.
2. On an HDR display, leave **HDR output** on **Auto — HDR when the display supports it** in **Settings › Display & HDR**.
3. On an SDR display, Kaveo fits the picture to it by itself.

## Good to know

- On an HDR display the picture is fitted to your screen's own peak, so it is neither clipped nor
  compressed; a brighter screen shows it brighter.
- On an SDR display it is treated as a 1000-nit master and tone-mapped, like an HDR film.
- A phone video with Dolby Vision plays with its Dolby Vision information: see [Dolby Vision](dolby-vision.md).
- **Save HDR frame** keeps the picture in HLG.
