## What it is for

**Settings › Audio › Effects** shapes the sound on its way out: it corrects your headphones or your
room, lets headphones sound like speakers, or runs an audio plugin.

![Settings › Audio › Effects: Effects on with 3 of 3 stages, Chain for on Every output playing on Headphones, the Everyday preset Made for Anything, the line from what plays to Headphones, and three cards — Headphone correction from an AutoEQ profile, Crossfeed and Room correction from REW — above Add stage](../images/settings/effects.webp)

## How to use it

1. Turn on **Effects**.
2. Start with **Correct my headphones**, **Correct my room** or **Crossfeed**, or use **Add stage**.
3. Open a card for its settings; its switch turns that stage off and keeps it.
4. **Save as new…** keeps the chain as a preset; **Made for** lets music and films choose their own.
5. To give one output its own chain, press the button under **Chain for** while it plays.

## Good to know

- The line above the cards shows the way the sound goes, and a card says when a stage cannot act on what plays.
- With **Effects** off the stages stay editable, and nothing is heard from them.
- Everything waits for **Save** ([Saving changes](saving.md)).
