## What it is for

**Settings › Audio › Effects** shapes the sound on its way out: it corrects your headphones or your
room, lets headphones sound like speakers, or runs an audio plugin.

![Settings › Audio › Effects: Effects on with 3 of 3 stages, Chain for on Every output playing on Headphones, the Everyday preset Made for Anything, the line from what plays to Headphones, and four cards — the Equalizer first, flat and off, then Headphone correction from an AutoEQ profile, Crossfeed and Room correction from REW](../images/settings/effects.webp)

## How to use it

1. Turn on **Effects**.
2. The **Equalizer** card is always first: its switch turns the curve on, **Preset** picks one of the
   built-in curves or one of yours, and **Edit bands** opens its ten bands (two columns). Bass, Treble
   and Tilt sit on top of the preset and stay when another preset is chosen.
3. Start with **Correct my headphones**, **Correct my room** ([Room correction](room-correction.md)) or
   **Crossfeed**, or use **Add stage** (for a plugin, see [Plugins](plugins.md)).
4. Open a card for its settings; its switch turns that stage off and keeps it.
5. **Save as new…** keeps the chain as a preset; **Made for** lets music and films choose their own.
6. To give one output its own chain, press the button under **Chain for** while it plays.

## Good to know

- The line above the cards shows the way the sound goes, and a card says when a stage cannot act on what plays.
- With **Effects** off the stages stay editable, and nothing is heard from them — the equalizer included,
  because it follows the chain. The same curve is also on Settings › Audio › General for now.
- The Equalizer card cannot be moved, removed or added twice.
- Everything waits for **Save** ([Saving changes](saving.md)).
