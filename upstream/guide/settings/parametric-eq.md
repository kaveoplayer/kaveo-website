## What it is for

A parametric EQ card — made by **Correct my headphones…**, **Correct my room…**, or **Add stage** › **Parametric EQ** — shapes the sound with filters at any frequency, each with its own gain and width.

## How to use it

1. Open the card and press **Edit bands**: each row is a filter, and **Channels** says which speakers it shapes (**All** by default).
2. Each channel's response is drawn in its own colour, with a legend; a band limited by **Channels** appears on its channel's curve.
3. Drag a point on the curve to move its band; hold Shift while dragging to change its width (**Q**).
4. **Copy filters** puts the card on the clipboard as EqualizerAPO text; **Save filters…** writes it to a file.
5. **Add stage** › **Paste filters…** reads such text back into a new card.

## Good to know

- What is copied is what plays: the automatic preamp travels with it, and a band that is off is left out.
- The table is the precise way to set a value; the points are the quick one.
- A saved profile opens in AutoEQ, REW or EqualizerAPO.
