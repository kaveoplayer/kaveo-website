## What it is for

**Settings › Shortcuts** lists the player's actions with the keys and mouse inputs that trigger
them, and is where you change them.
What each action ships with is [Keyboard shortcuts](../keyboard-shortcuts.md).

## How to use it

1. Each row is one action this platform has, with its shortcuts beside it — what they do is
   [Player controls](../watching/player-controls.md).
2. Click **Add** on a row — it asks you to *press a key or mouse…* — and press what you want.
   **Cancel**, or **Esc**, backs out.
3. Click the cross beside a shortcut to drop it: an action can hold several, so the rest stay.
4. **Reset to defaults**, at the bottom, puts every action back to what it shipped with.
5. Click **Save** to keep any of it — see [Saving changes](saving.md).

## Good to know

- A key or button belongs to one action: give it to another and it is taken from the first, with no
  warning.
- A shortcut can be a mouse button or a wheel notch (**Wheel ↑**) as readily as a key, and it may
  carry **Ctrl**, **Shift** or **Alt**.
- Four actions arrive with no shortcut at all. **Esc** and **F12** are not in the list because they
  work on every screen, not only over a film.
