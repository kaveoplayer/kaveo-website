## What it is for

**Settings › Picture** covers the picture as a whole: its colour, and where and at what shape it
is drawn.

![Settings shown on the Picture section: the rail listing all sixteen sections with Picture selected first under Video & display, and beside it Colour adjustment — Brightness at 0.00, Contrast, Saturation and Gamma at 1.00, Hue at 0°, Colour LUT (.cube) on None with Choose… — then Picture placement — Aspect ratio on From the file, Pan and scan at 0.00, Zoom at 1.00×, Pan horizontally and Pan vertically at 0.00.](../images/settings/picture.webp)

## How to use it

1. Under **Colour adjustment**, move **Brightness**, **Contrast**, **Saturation**, **Hue** and
   **Gamma**; the round arrow at the end of a row puts that one back — see
   [Restoring defaults](restoring-defaults.md).
2. **Colour LUT (.cube)** applies a grading look from a file you pick with **Choose…** — a look, not
   screen calibration. The cross beside the file appears once one is set, and **LUT interpretation** is
   greyed out until then.
3. Under **Picture placement**, set **Aspect ratio** — **From the file** to begin with — when a film
   is drawn at the wrong shape.
4. Raise **Pan and scan** to trade black bars for cropped edges and **Zoom** to enlarge;
   **Pan horizontally** and **Pan vertically** shift the picture inside the window.
   **Restore defaults** puts colour and placement back at once.
5. Click **Save** to keep any of it — see [Saving changes](saving.md).

## Good to know

- They re-grade; they do not repair a compressed or banded source — that is the Upscaler section's
  **Source** tab.
- **Pan horizontally** and **Pan vertically** move the picture whether or not anything is cropped:
  where there are black bars it slides into them, and otherwise it slides off one edge, leaving black
  behind it. The round arrow at the end of each row brings it back.
- Nothing is kept until **Save**, though a film on screen follows every change at once, resets
  included.
