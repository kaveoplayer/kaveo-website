## What it is for

Kaveo can tell a service what you played, so that record lives somewhere besides this computer: ListenBrainz keeps the music you listen to, Trakt and Simkl the films and episodes you watch.

## How to use it

1. Open **Settings**, then **Connections**.
2. Switch on **Scrobbling**, then the service you want.
3. For ListenBrainz, copy your user token from listenbrainz.org/settings into **Token**, then choose **Connect**.
4. For Trakt, choose **Connect**, open trakt.tv/activate and type the code the page shows.
5. For Simkl, choose **Connect** and authorize Kaveo in the browser window that opens; Kaveo takes the answer back by itself.
6. Choose **Save** at the bottom of the screen.

## Good to know

- A track is reported once half of it has played, or four minutes, whichever comes first; a film or episode is reported as you watch, and past 80 % Trakt and Simkl count it as watched.
- More than one service can be on: a film is reported to each watch service you connected, a track to the music services.
- **Disconnect** removes the sign-in and nothing is sent afterwards. If a service refuses — an email address it has not verified, for instance — the sentence says why in the service's own words.
- A hidden title, an address you opened and a disc are never reported.
