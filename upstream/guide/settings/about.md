## What it is for

**Settings › About** shows the version you are running and whether a newer Kaveo is out.

![Settings shown on the About section: the rail listing all fifteen sections with About selected last under App, and beside it the Version row, the Updates row with Check automatically ticked and a Check now button, and the line Not checked yet.](../images/settings/about.webp)

## How to use it

1. Read **Version** and quote it in a bug report, alongside a bundle from
   [Diagnostics](diagnostics.md).
2. **Check automatically** asks once at start and once a day; click **Check now** to ask at once.
3. When a newer Kaveo is out, a badge with its version appears in the top bar. Click **Update now**:
   Kaveo downloads it, checks it, closes and starts again on the new version. **What's new** shows its
   notes; **Later** hides the badge for that version.

## Good to know

- The check sends the Kaveo version and nothing else. Untick **Check automatically** and click
  **Save** to stop it; **Check now** still works.
- If a film is playing, the update waits until it ends. Installed with a package, Kaveo asks for
  your password once, as your system's own updates do.
- A copy that was not installed from a release says so, and does not update itself.
- See [Troubleshooting](../troubleshooting.md) for what to send with a report.
