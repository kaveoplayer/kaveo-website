## What it is for

**Settings › About** shows the version you are running, whether a newer Kaveo is out, and where to
report a problem or ask a question.

![Settings shown on the About section: the rail listing all sixteen sections with About selected last under App, and beside it the Version row, the Updates row with Check automatically ticked and a Check now button, the line Not checked yet, and the Help row with Report a problem and Ask a question buttons.](../images/settings/about.webp)

## How to use it

1. **Check automatically** asks once at start and once a day; click **Check now** to ask at once.
2. When a newer Kaveo is out, a badge with its version appears in the top bar. Click **Update now**:
   Kaveo downloads it, checks it, closes and starts again on the new version. **What's new** shows its
   notes; **Later** hides the badge for that version.
3. **Report a problem…** opens the issue form on GitHub in your browser, with the version, your
   system, graphics card and components already filled in. **Ask a question** opens Discussions.

## Good to know

- The check sends the Kaveo version and nothing else. Untick **Check automatically** and click
  **Save** to stop it; **Check now** still works.
- If a film is playing, the update waits until it ends. Installed with a package, Kaveo asks for
  your password once, as your system's own updates do.
- A copy that was not installed from a release says so, and does not update itself.
- A newer build of a component Kaveo installed is named here too; update it in
  [Components](components.md).
- The report form never receives a file or folder name, and nothing is sent until you submit it
  there. Attach a bundle from [Diagnostics](diagnostics.md) — see
  [Troubleshooting](../troubleshooting.md).
