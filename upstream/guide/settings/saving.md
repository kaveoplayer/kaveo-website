## What it is for

Every settings page shares one Save and Revert bar, so you always know whether a change is only on
screen or already kept.

![The bar across the bottom of the settings screen after one option was changed: it reads Unsaved changes, you are seeing them, they are not kept yet, with Revert and Save live at the right-hand end.](../images/settings/saving-bar.webp)

## How to use it

1. Open a settings page — what you change takes effect right away, so you can see the picture, the
   sound or the language change as you go.
2. Click **Save** when you are happy with it. Nothing reaches disk before that.
3. Click **Revert** to put things back the way they were — every unsaved change, not only this
   page's.
4. If you leave with something unsaved — the back arrow, the top bar, the quick panel over the
   player, or closing Kaveo — you are asked to **Save**, **Discard**, or **Stay**.

## Good to know

- Not everything waits for **Save**. On **Settings › Library**, **Categories** applies and rescans
  at once and **Clear history** empties the list — **Revert** undoes neither. Nor does an action on
  your files or the outside world wait: importing, **Recatalogue library**, connecting a server,
  **Start recording** and **Export a diagnostic bundle** all happen the moment you click. The one
  tick that does not wait is **File manager integration**, on [Interface](interface.md); and no
  row at all on [Diagnostics](diagnostics.md) waits.
- The quick panel over the player (**Ctrl+H**) shows some of the same settings, with the same
  Save/Revert bar.
- See [Diagnostics](diagnostics.md) for logs, recordings and a bundle to send when something goes
  wrong.
