## What it is for

**Settings › Subtitles** decides whether subtitles are drawn and how they look, and holds the
credentials that translate or fetch them. Its three sub-pages — **Appearance**, **Translation** and
**Online search** — appear in the rail and as tabs.

![Settings with Subtitles chosen in the rail and its three sub-pages listed under it, repeated as tabs above the Appearance page: Render subtitles ticked, Preferred languages empty and showing its faint example en, it, ja, Forced captions only unticked, Style override on Off (keep the script's typeset), and Position on As the file says](../images/settings/subtitles.webp)

## How to use it

1. **Appearance** holds **Render subtitles**, which switches the rest of the tab on and off;
   **Preferred languages**, the codes a track is chosen by until you pick one yourself;
   **Forced captions only**, for signs and foreign dialogue; **Style override**; and **Position** —
   see [Position and style](../subtitles/position-and-style.md).
2. **Translation** holds **Provider**, **Endpoint**, **Model**, **Translate into**,
   **Read the original audio**, **Re-read the translation** and an **API key** — see
   [Translating subtitles](../subtitles/translate.md) and
   [Translating on your own computer](../subtitles/local-models.md). Names and speakers are set on
   the subtitle — see [Better translations](../subtitles/translation-quality.md).
3. **Online search** holds OpenSubtitles' **API key**, **Languages**, **Account** and **Password**
   — see [Finding subtitles online](../subtitles/online-search.md).

## Good to know

- **Style override** on **Off (keep the script's typeset)** draws no styling rows; **Size only**
  adds **Text size**; and **Font, colour, outline and shadow** keeps that one and adds four more —
  **Font family**, **Text colour**, **Outline width** and **Shadow depth**.
- **Appearance** is read when a title opens, so a change made while a film is on screen reloads
  it briefly, at the same place.
- Everything here waits for **Save**, the two keys and the password included — see
  [Saving changes](saving.md).
