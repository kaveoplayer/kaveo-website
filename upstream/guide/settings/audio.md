## What it is for

**Settings › Audio** decides where the sound goes, whether Kaveo takes that output for itself, and
how special formats reach it.

![Settings with Audio chosen under Playback in the rail, and beside it the caption How the sound leaves the machine, then Play audio on, Exclusive output off, Bitstream passthrough on, DSD output set to Automatic with the note that DSD is converted to PCM here, and Downmix to stereo set to Automatic](../images/settings/audio.webp)

## How to use it

1. **Play audio** is on to begin with.
2. Turn on **Exclusive output** for bit-perfect sound that bypasses the system mixer.
3. **Bitstream passthrough** sends Dolby and DTS to a receiver untouched, decoded where it cannot.
4. **DSD output** decides how DSF and DFF files reach a DAC: **Automatic (native DSD, else PCM)**,
   **DoP (DSD over PCM)**, **Native DSD** or **Convert to PCM**. DSD goes out as it is only to a
   chosen **Audio output device** with **Exclusive output** on.
5. **Downmix to stereo**: **Automatic** folds only when the output has fewer channels than the film.
6. Pick an **Audio output device**, or leave **System default**.

## Good to know

- Choose **DoP (DSD over PCM)** only for a DAC that decodes it: other devices play it as noise.
  While DSD goes to the DAC, volume, the equalizer, crossfade and the visualizer do not apply.
- Pinning a device makes **Automatic** downmix stand down; **Use a different setting for this
  output** gives it a rule instead.
- Everything waits for **Save** ([Saving changes](saving.md)); see also
  [Volume and dynamics](../watching/volume-and-dynamics.md) and
  [Listening to music](../watching/listen-to-music.md).
