## What it is for

**Settings › Audio** decides where the sound goes, how special formats reach it, and which
soundtrack a film opens on.

![Settings with Audio chosen in the rail: Play audio on, Exclusive output off, Bitstream passthrough on, DSD output with its note that DSD is converted to PCM here, and Downmix to stereo on Automatic](../images/settings/audio.webp)

## How to use it

1. **Exclusive output** with a chosen **Audio output device** is bit-perfect, bypassing the mixer.
2. **Bitstream passthrough** sends Dolby and DTS to a receiver untouched.
3. **DSD output**: how DSF and DFF files reach a DAC; as DSD only to a chosen
   **Audio output device** with **Exclusive output** on.
4. **Downmix to stereo**: **Automatic** folds only for an output narrower than the film (an HDMI
   receiver is asked).
5. **Volume control** on **Output device** keeps the sound bit-perfect below 100 %
   ([Volume and dynamics](../watching/volume-and-dynamics.md)).
6. **Sync offset for this output** corrects a late output, like Bluetooth headphones.
7. **Preferred audio languages**: **Add language**, best first; drag to reorder.
8. **Keep pitch when changing speed**: off, faster sounds higher, like a tape.

## Good to know

- Choose **DoP (DSD over PCM)** only for a DAC that decodes it: others play noise. While DSD
  goes to the DAC, volume, the equalizer, crossfade and the visualizer do not apply.
- A sample rate the device lacks is converted, and Kaveo says so.
- A title opens on the first of those languages it has, unless its series has one; never on a
  commentary or an audio description.
- Pinning a device other than HDMI makes **Automatic** downmix stand down; **Use a different
  setting for this output** gives it a rule.
- Everything waits for **Save** ([Saving changes](saving.md)).
