## What it is for

**Settings › Interface** covers how Kaveo looks and behaves, and whether it appears in your
desktop's menus and media controls.

![Settings shown on the Interface section: the rail listing all sixteen sections with Interface selected under App, and beside it Language on English, App theme and Player theme on Dark, Colour from the cover on, the accent swatches on Theme's, Interface size 100 %, Text size Default, Control size Compact, Hover preview on Clip from the video, Trailer quality and language, and Featured background on Gradient rather than the default Title artwork, above the Player group's heading.](../images/settings/interface.webp)

## How to use it

1. Pick a **Language**: **English**, **Italiano**, **Français** or **Español**.
2. Choose an **App theme** for the library and a **Player theme** for what surrounds a film, from
   **Dark** (the default), **Light**, **Pure black (OLED)**, **High contrast**, **Warm night** and
   **Indigo**.
   **Colour from the cover** tints a song's screen; **Light** and **High contrast** stay plain.
3. Pick an **Accent colour**, an **Interface size**, a **Text size** and a **Control size**.
4. Set **Hover preview** to **Clip from the video** or **YouTube trailer**; a server's title
   always plays its trailer.
5. Set **Trailer quality** and **Trailer language**; see [Trailers](../library/trailers.md).
6. Choose a **Featured background** — **Title artwork** is the default; see
   [The library home](../library/home.md).
7. Leave **System media controls** on for the media keys; see
   [Media keys and the desktop](../watching/media-keys.md). Turn on **File manager integration** to put
   Kaveo in your desktop's right-click menus; see [From your file manager](../file-manager.md).
8. Turn off **Seek bar previews** to show only the time and chapter.
9. Set **Slideshow interval** per photo; see [Slideshow](../library/slideshow.md).

## Good to know

- Every row but **File manager integration** waits for **Save**; the language, the themes,
  **System media controls** and **Seek bar previews** change at once; **Revert** undoes them.
- That one acts at once, and **Revert** does not undo it.
