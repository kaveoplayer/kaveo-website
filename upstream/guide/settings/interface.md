## What it is for

**Settings › Interface** covers how Kaveo looks and behaves, and its place in your
desktop's menus and media controls.

![Settings on Interface › General: the General · Layout · Themes bar over Language, Themes with the app's and the player's theme and Choose…, Colour from the cover, Player controls opacity, Accent colour, Interface size, Text size and Control size, then Hover preview and the two trailer rows](../images/settings/interface.webp)

## How to use it

1. Pick a **Language**: **English**, **Italiano**, **Français** or **Español**.
2. **Themes** names the app's and the player's theme; **Choose…** opens
   [Themes](themes.md). **Colour from the cover** tints a song's screen; **Light** and
   **High contrast** stay plain.
3. Pick an **Accent colour**, an **Interface size**, a **Text size**, a **Control size** and a
   **Player controls opacity**.
4. Set **Hover preview** to **Clip from the video** or **YouTube trailer**; a server's title
   always plays its trailer.
5. Set **Trailer quality** and **Trailer language**; see [Trailers](../library/trailers.md).
6. Choose a **Featured background** (default **Title artwork**); see
   [The library home](../library/home.md).
7. Leave **System media controls** on for the media keys
   ([Media keys and the desktop](../watching/media-keys.md)). **File manager integration** puts Kaveo
   in your right-click menus ([From your file manager](../file-manager.md)).
8. **Keep the screen on for music** stops the screen sleeping during songs.
9. Turn off **Seek bar previews** for only the time and chapter.
10. Set **Slideshow interval** per photo; see [Slideshow](../library/slideshow.md).

## Good to know

- The language, the themes, **System media controls**, **Keep the screen on for music**,
  **Seek bar previews** and **File manager integration** change at once; the rest waits for
  **Save**. **Revert** undoes all but the last.
