## What it is for

**Settings › Behaviour** holds four rows: the player's two decisions about skipping a series' opening
or ending, the format of a frame you save from the player, and how sharp a video opened from a web
address plays.

![Settings with Behaviour chosen under Playback in the rail, and the section's four rows — Offer to skip intros and endings, on, Find intros and endings from the audio, on, Saved frames with JPEG chosen, and Web video quality on Automatic](../images/watching/skip-intro-setting.webp)

## How to use it

1. **Offer to skip intros and endings** is on to begin with. See
   [Skipping openings](../watching/skip-intro.md) for what the button does.
2. **Find intros and endings from the audio** is on to begin with: it is what lets the button appear
   on an episode whose chapters name nothing. See [Skipping openings](../watching/skip-intro.md).
3. **Saved frames** is **JPEG** to begin with; choose **PNG** for pictures that lose nothing. See
   [Saving a frame](../watching/save-a-frame.md).
4. **Web video quality** is **Automatic** to begin with: the sharpest the stream offers, up to your
   screen's height. Choose a height to cap it there; the player's right-click **Quality** menu
   changes it for the video playing.
5. Click **Save** to keep a change — see [Saving changes](saving.md).

## Good to know

- Some photo apps read a picture's date only from a JPEG, and show none for a PNG frame.
