## What it is for

**Settings › Display & HDR** decides what your screen is sent, and how an HDR film is fitted to it.

![Settings, Display & HDR selected in the rail with its HDR output, Tone mapping and Format & refresh sub-items. Beside it the Output group — HDR output on Auto, Use HDR on SDR content off, Display peak on Auto with a note that both apply live, then Disc subtitle brightness in HDR on As on the disc — and Signal & calibration with Mastering peak, Mastering black and Calibration LUT. The three tabs and the Per-display settings group are above the picture.](../images/settings/display.webp)

## How to use it

1. **Editing settings of** picks the screen; **Create a profile for this display**, **Copy values from** and **Delete profile** manage them.
2. **HDR output** holds **Output**: **HDR output**, **Use HDR on SDR content** — off, so only an HDR
   film switches the screen, see [HDR output](../watching/hdr.md) — **Display peak** and **Disc
   subtitle brightness in HDR**; then
   **Signal & calibration**: **Mastering peak**, **Mastering black** and **Calibration LUT** (grading is
   [Picture](picture.md)). **Override** in a value's menu sets your own.
3. **Tone mapping** holds **SDR tone-map target**, **SDR tone-map black**, **Tone-map curve**,
   **Gamut mapping**, **HDR metadata**, **Contrast recovery**, **Mastering clip**, **Film response**
   and **Inverse tone mapping**, then
   **Dynamic tone-mapping (peak detection)**: **Peak detection**, **Peak percentile**, **Smoothing
   period**, **Scene-change threshold (low)** and **Scene-change threshold (high)**.
4. **Format & refresh** holds **Output (bit depth & gamut)**: **Bit depth** (SDR only), **Output range**,
   **Output gamut**, **Picture gamma**; **Display refresh**: **Match
   refresh to content**, **Refresh policy**; **Presentation**: **Lighter presentation**, **Full brightness in fullscreen**.

## Good to know

- A screen with no profile uses **Global (displays without a profile)**; a saved one returns when the
  player moves to that screen, and one Kaveo cannot identify gets none.
- **Disc subtitle brightness in HDR** is for a disc's own subtitles, not text ones.
- Nothing is kept until **Save** — see [Saving changes](saving.md) — which is also when the screen
  switches into or out of HDR.
