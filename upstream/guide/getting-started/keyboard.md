## What it is for

Kaveo works without a pointer: one key moves between the controls on the screen, another presses the
one it is on, and a search finds a command you cannot see.

## How to use it

1. Press **Tab** to move to the next control and **Shift+Tab** to go back. The control you are on is
   outlined, and its label is read aloud by a screen reader.
2. Press **Enter** or **Space** to press it. In a row of cards the arrow keys move between them, and
   in the player they seek and set the volume.
3. Press **Esc** to leave a menu, a dialog or the player, and to go back from a page.
4. Press **Ctrl+K** anywhere to search the commands of the screen you are on. Type a few letters,
   choose with the arrow keys, and press **Enter**.
5. Press **Shift+F10** or the **Menu** key for the context menu of the card or row you are on.

## Good to know

- A command that cannot act right now is still listed, greyed, with the reason under it.
- The player's own keys are on [Keyboard shortcuts](../keyboard-shortcuts.md); **Tab** reaches its
  bar while it is on screen.
- Notices are announced as they appear, so nothing is said only in passing.
