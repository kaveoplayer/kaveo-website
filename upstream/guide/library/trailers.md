## What it is for

Watch a film's or a series' trailer without leaving Kaveo, as sharp as your screen allows, in the
language you prefer.

## How to use it

1. On a title's page, click **Trailer**. It plays over the page; click outside to close it.
2. In **Settings › Interface**, set **Trailer quality**: **Automatic** follows your screen and
   connection, so a trailer starts within seconds; a fixed quality, up to **2160p (4K)**, is
   downloaded however long it takes.
3. Set **Trailer language** to **Same as the app**, **Original language of the title** or one
   language.
4. To see trailers on covers, set **Hover preview** to **YouTube trailer**.

## Good to know

- The first play downloads the trailer (a few seconds); after that it starts at once.
- With no trailer in the chosen language, one in another language plays.
- A trailer kept beside a film plays first: name it like the film with *-trailer* added
  (*Film (2016)-trailer.mkv*), or put it in a *Trailers* folder. It never becomes a title of its own.
- Many trailers exist only in 1080p, so a 4K screen shows them at 1080p.
- A Jellyfin title plays the trailer the server keeps or knows, or one found for it.
- When nothing else has one, Kaveo asks AniList (for anime) and KinoCheck, free trailer services.
  When neither has one, a short clip cut from the title itself plays instead — behind **Trailer**
  and on a cover's hover preview. A title on a Jellyfin server is cut from its stream.
