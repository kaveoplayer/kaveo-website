## What it is for

Watch a film's or a series' trailer without leaving Kaveo, as sharp as your screen allows and in the
language you prefer.

## How to use it

1. On a title's page, click **Trailer**. It plays over the page; click outside it to close it.
2. In **Settings › Interface**, set **Trailer quality**. **Automatic** follows the screen Kaveo is on
   and your connection, so a trailer starts within seconds. A fixed quality, up to **2160p (4K)**, is
   downloaded however long it takes.
3. Set **Trailer language** to **Same as the app**, **Original language of the title** or one
   language.
4. To see trailers on the library's covers, set **Hover preview** to **YouTube trailer**.

## Good to know

- The first time a trailer plays it is downloaded, which takes a few seconds; after that it starts
  at once.
- When a title has no trailer in the chosen language, its trailer in another language plays.
- A trailer you keep beside a film plays first: name it like the film with *-trailer* added
  (*Film (2016)-trailer.mkv*), or put it in a *Trailers* folder in the film's or the series' folder.
  It is never shown as a title of its own.
- Many trailers are published only in 1080p, so a 4K screen shows them at 1080p.
- A title on a Jellyfin server plays the trailer the server keeps or knows, or one found for it.
- When nothing else has one, Kaveo asks KinoCheck, a free trailer service.
