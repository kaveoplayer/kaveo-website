## What it is for

A personal category holds your own photos and videos, from a phone or a camera, grouped by the
folders you already made, newest first. They never appear on the home or among films and series.

![The Home videos category: one card per folder, each saying how many photos and videos it holds and when](../images/library/personal-videos-page.webp)

## How to use it

1. In **Settings › Library › Categories**, add a category, tick **Personal**, then click
   **Add folder…** and pick the folder of your videos.
2. Click the category at the top of the library, then a folder to open it.
3. A folder shows its photos and videos together, newest first. Click a video to play it, or
   **Play all** — each video plays into the next.
4. Click a photo to open it on its own — see [Viewing photos](view-photos.md).

![A folder's page: its photos and videos in one grid, newest first, each video with its length](../images/library/personal-videos-folder.webp)

## Good to know

- Kaveo never renames, moves or looks up your videos online, and never files a download into this
  category.
- A photo or video's date is the one the camera wrote, or else the file's own.
- A Live Photo carries a **LIVE** badge; its short video stays with it rather than appearing on its
  own.
- AVCHD camera files are not shown yet.
