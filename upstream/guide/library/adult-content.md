## What it is for

Hide titles tagged adult from every library screen — the rows, the grid, search and
*Recently played* — without touching the files themselves.

![The Adult content group in Settings, with Show adult content on](../images/library/adult-content-settings.webp)

## How to use it

1. Mark a title from its own card: **Mark as Adult** on a film, a series, or one of your own photos or videos.
2. Mark a whole folder of your own footage with **Mark folder as adult** on the folder's card.
3. Open **Settings › Library › Folders**, find **Adult content** and turn off **Show adult content**.
4. Click **Save**, and turn the switch back on when you want the titles to reappear.

## Good to know

- Marking a series covers every episode; marking a folder covers everything inside it.
- It is a visibility switch, not a lock — Settings stays reachable and nothing is encrypted.
- To keep a title really private, move it into the [vault](../private/index.md).
