## What it is for

On a series' page, Kaveo tells you which episodes of each season you are missing, and what is still to
air.

## How to use it

1. Open a series' page and look under each season's heading for *Missing episodes:* and its numbers.
2. Check the top of the page for the next episode airing, and each season's heading for its own
   schedule.
3. If your numbering does not match the provider's, click the offer under the message if it is right,
   for example *This season starts at my episode 14*.
4. Click **Follow** so the show appears on your home screen in *Coming soon* or *Catch up*.

## Good to know

- Kaveo stays quiet rather than guess — nothing shows for specials, an unmatched show, or a season you
  own none of.
- An episode that has not aired yet is never counted as missing.
- A file covering a whole season, like a ripped Blu-ray disc, counts as present for every episode on
  it.
