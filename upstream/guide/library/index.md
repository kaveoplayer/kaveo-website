Everything Kaveo finds in your folders and on your servers is here — browse it, split it into places
of your own, and correct what it got wrong.

- [The library home](home.md) — the featured band, the rows, and new files.
- [The card menu](title-menu.md) — quick actions from a right-click on a title's card.
- [Trailers](trailers.md) — play a trailer, and choose its quality and language.
- [Search and genres](search.md) — find a title, or narrow the library to one genre.
- [Artists](artists.md) — browse music by artist.
- [Playlists](playlists.md) — your own lists of songs.
- [Categories](categories.md) — split the library into places of your own.
- [Genres and tags](tags.md) — see and correct a title's genres and its anime or adult tag.
- [Hiding adult content](adult-content.md) — keep adult titles out of the library screens.
- [Correcting a wrong match](correct-a-match.md) — fix a title matched to the wrong film or
  series.
- [Collections](collections.md) — group the films and series of one saga.
- [Missing episodes](missing-episodes.md) — the episodes of a season you lack, and what is coming.
- [Metadata files](metadata-files.md) — read and write the NFO files of other media tools.
- [Your own photos and videos](personal-videos.md) — phone and camera photos and videos, in a
  place of their own.
- [Viewing photos](view-photos.md) — your own photos one at a time, zoomed in or not.
- [Slideshow](slideshow.md) — a folder's photos and videos, one after another.
- [Exporting your photos and videos](export-your-videos.md) — copy them, with their dates.
