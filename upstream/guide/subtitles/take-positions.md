## What it is for

A subtitle in your language, often an SRT, shows every sign at the bottom. If the video has an ASS
track that knows where each sign sits, Kaveo can give your subtitle those places and italics.

## How to use it

### While it plays

1. Right-click the picture, open **Subtitles** › **Tools** and point at **Take positions from…**.
2. Pick an ASS track.
3. Read how many lines move, then choose **Replace** or **Make a copy**.

### For a film, an episode, a season or a series

1. Open the **⋯** menu of its page, season or episode and choose **Take subtitle positions…**.
2. Choose the language to place and the track to take the positions from.
3. Choose **Replace each subtitle** or **Make a copy of each**, then **Take positions**.

## Good to know

- Only a line that matches one line of the track moves. A line the translation splits, joins or
  leaves out stays where it is, and no word changes.
- Kaveo copies the track's choices, including dialogue it puts at the top.
- **Replace** cannot be undone; **Make a copy** keeps your subtitle in the list.
