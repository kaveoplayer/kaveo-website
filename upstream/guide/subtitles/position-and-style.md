## What it is for

Move a subtitle line, change how a style looks, or move every subtitle at once.

![The Styles section of an ASS subtitle, open on its one style, Default: Font, reading Arial with a red not installed beside it, then Size, a Text and an Outline colour, Outline width, Shadow, Bold, Italic and Margins L/R/V](../images/subtitles/position-and-style-styles.webp)

## How to use it

### Place and style a line

1. Only ASS can be placed and styled, so for an SRT or WebVTT file first click **Convert to ASS** in
   the editor or on **All lines…**. Kaveo plays an ASS copy; the original stays.
2. In the editor, click a cell of the *Place* grid, type **X** and **Y**, or drag the line on the
   picture.
3. On **All lines…**, open **Styles** to change a style's font, size, colours, outline or margins;
   every line using it follows.

### Move every subtitle

![Settings › Subtitles › Appearance: Style override on Off (keep the script's typeset), and Position with its grid of nine cells and As the file says selected](../images/subtitles/position-and-style-settings.webp)

1. Pick a cell under **Position** in **Settings › Subtitles › Appearance** and click **Save**.
   **As the file says**, then **Save**, undoes it.

## Good to know

- A style change reaches the picture when you click **Back**.
- If a style change does nothing, set **Style override** in **Settings › Subtitles › Appearance** back
  to **Off (keep the script's typeset)**.
- A red *not installed* beside a font means this computer does not have it, so the player quietly
  substitutes another and the result will not look like what you set.
