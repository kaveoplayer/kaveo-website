## What it is for

Pick a translation model for the quality you want and the price you accept.

## How to use it

1. In **Settings › Subtitles › Translation**, choose **OpenRouter** as **Provider** and paste its
   **API key**.
2. In **Model**, type part of the model's name and pick it from the list.
3. Tick **Re-read the translation** only with Claude Sonnet 5.5, then press **Save**.

## Which model

- Claude Sonnet 5.5 with **Re-read the translation**: the fewest mistakes on the hard lines, such as
  who is a man or a woman. About 5–10 lines to correct an episode; 0.88 $ an episode, 11 $ for 13
  episodes, 23 $ for 26.
- DeepSeek V4.1 Flash: about 5–10 lines; two minutes and 0.03 $ an episode, under 1 $ for 26.
  The best value.
- Kimi K3: about 10 lines; 0.34 $ an episode, 9 $ for 26.
- Gemini 2.5 Flash: about 20 lines; under two minutes an episode, 0.05 $.
- A local model in Ollama: about 35 lines; free, and nothing leaves your computer.

## Good to know

- Measured in October 2026 on one 45-minute anime episode, English into Italian, against a
  translation corrected by hand. Prices and models change.
- Grok took over half an hour for one episode. DeepSeek V4 Pro and V4 Flash made more mistakes than
  V4.1 Flash.
