## What it is for

Fix a subtitle's timing or words against the film, or write a new one.

![The All lines… page: Back and the subtitle's name, with Put in time order, Undo, Redo and Save; a Find box with Only translated lines and Only lines that do not fit, and a count of 60 lines; Break the row (7) and Give time (3); a note that this subtitle has no styles table, with Convert to ASS; Names and terms (0), Who is speaking (0), and How this title should sound with its Natural, Literal and Keep honorifics presets and a free-text box; then the first lines of the subtitle, each with its number, its start and end, and its words](../images/subtitles/edit-all-lines.webp)

## How to use it

1. With a subtitle file on screen, right-click the picture, open **Subtitles** and choose
   **Edit subtitles…**, or press **E**.
2. Click **Start = now [** or **End = now ]**, or press **[** or **]**, to set the line's start or end
   to the frame on screen. **↑** and **↓** move between lines.
3. Click in the line's box to change its words — **Shift+Enter** starts a new line — then press
   **Enter** or click outside it.
4. Click **All lines…** to correct lines in a list.
5. Or choose **Write one from nothing…** in **Subtitles** to start with one empty line.

## Good to know

- A track inside the video has to be extracted first — see [Loading subtitles](load.md).
- The panel saves as you go and on **Done**; the **All lines…** page saves on **Back**.
- While the panel is open, the playback bar is its top row. Drag the panel's top edge for more lines
  or more picture.
- To remove a subtitle Kaveo keeps, click its bin under *Subtitle files*; Kaveo asks first. A file
  you put beside the video yourself is never changed.
