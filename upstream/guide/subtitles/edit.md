## What it is for

Fix a subtitle's timing or words against the film, or write a new one.

![The All lines… page: Back and the subtitle's name marked only in Kaveo, with Put in time order, Undo, Redo, Save as… and Save; a Find box with Only translated lines and Only lines that do not fit, and a count of 60 lines; Break the row (7) and Give time (3); a note that this subtitle has no styles table, with Convert to ASS; Names and terms (0), Who is speaking (0), and How this title should sound with its Natural, Literal and Keep honorifics presets and a free-text box; then the first lines of the subtitle, each with its number, its start and end, and its words](../images/subtitles/edit-all-lines.webp)

## How to use it

![The subtitle editor under the picture: the playback bar on top, then Editing en marked only in Kaveo, with Follow the film, Undo, Redo, All lines…, Done, Save as… and Save; Start = now and End = now, the four nudges, This line and all after → now, Add a line here, Remove this line and Pin this line here; the lines around the one on screen, which is highlighted; and on the right the Look column with This line, Style and Every subtitle, greyed because SubRip cannot carry a position or a font, above Convert to ASS](../images/subtitles/edit-panel.webp)

1. With a subtitle file on screen, right-click the picture, open **Subtitles** › **Tools** and
   choose **Edit subtitles…**, or press **E**.
2. Click **Start = now [** or **End = now ]**, or press **[** or **]**, to set the line's start or end
   to the frame on screen. **↑** and **↓** move between lines.
3. Click in the line's box to change its words — **Shift+Enter** starts a new line — then press
   **Enter** or click outside it.
4. Click **All lines…** to correct lines in a list.
5. Or choose **Write one from nothing…** in **Subtitles** › **Tools** to start with one empty line.

## Good to know

- A track inside the video has to be extracted first — see [Loading subtitles](load.md).
- Kaveo keeps your changes as you go; other players see them once you save the subtitle — see
  [Saving a subtitle](save.md).
- While the panel is open, the playback bar is its top row. Drag the panel's top edge for more lines
  or more picture.
- On the **All lines…** page, a line added from the audio has its times in colour, and **Only lines
  from the audio** shows just those.
- To remove a subtitle Kaveo keeps, click its bin under **Beside the video**; Kaveo asks first. A file
  you put beside the video yourself, or saved, is never changed.
