## What it is for

Watch and steer the subtitles waiting to be translated ([how to queue them](translation-queue.md)).

## How to use it

The top bar shows the queue's progress, for example *Translating 3/9 · 42%*. Click it to open the
queue.

![The Translation queue, paused with two left: a series paused after its first episode, with the Check names and people and Continue buttons and its episodes, a failed film with a retry button, a film waiting, a ⋯ on each, and the Resume, Clear finished and Close buttons](../images/subtitles/translation-queue-panel.webp)

- **Pause** stops after the request in progress; **Resume** goes on from there.
- A series paused after its first episode waits for you: **Check names and people…** opens the
  names and people it found. Correct them, press **Back to the queue**, then **Continue**.
- A series' **⋯** holds **Move to the front**, **Move up**, **Move down** and **Remove this series
  from the queue**; episodes keep their order.
- The cross cancels the episode in progress; the bin removes an episode.
- The round arrow translates an episode again, or retries one that failed.

## Good to know

- The top bar shows nothing when the queue is empty or everything went well.
- The player hides the top bar: go back to the library to see it.
- A running queue continues when Kaveo starts again.
- An episode marked in red needs you: hover over it to see why and what to do.
- A line under a finished episode names a style whose font is missing or lacks letters of your
  language: **Use another font for this style…** picks an installed one that has them.
- A grey line under a finished episode counts the lines added from the audio. Press **Review them…**
  to see only those, and delete any that is not dialogue.
