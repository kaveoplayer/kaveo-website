## What it is for

Kaveo can add itself to your desktop, so a file you have just downloaded is one right-click from
playing or being filed into your library.

![The File manager integration row at the foot of Settings › Interface, its switch off](images/file-manager/file-manager-settings.webp)

## How to use it

1. Turn on **File manager integration** in **Settings › Interface**
   ([Interface](settings/interface.md)). It acts at once.
2. Open a video, a track, a photo or a [playlist](library/playlists.md) with Kaveo, or pick **Add to Kaveo's queue** from its
   **Kaveo** submenu — see [Next episode and queue](watching/next-episode.md).
3. From that submenu, **Add to Movies (move)** files it into a category and renames it to match your
   library; **Add to Movies (link, keep the original)** leaves the original where it is.
4. On a folder, **Play this folder with Kaveo** plays its files in order, **Loop this folder with
   Kaveo** repeats them, and **Add to** files the whole folder.

## Good to know

- A photo opens in the [viewer](library/view-photos.md); the submenu is for videos and folders.
- The **Kaveo** submenu comes from KDE: Dolphin shows it, GNOME Files and Thunar do not, though
  opening a file with Kaveo still works there.
- If Kaveo moves or is installed again, turn the setting back on. Turning it off removes everything it added, and never makes Kaveo your default player.
- Filing happens at once; Kaveo asks only about an overwrite. Only categories that accept films or series are offered. A video in a
  personal category's folder still gets them, but Kaveo refuses and says why.
