## What it is for

Removing a connection takes its folders out of your library without forgetting where you stopped,
what you watched or your history on it.

## How to use it

1. Click **Sources**, then **Remove** on the connection's card.
2. Read which folders leave the library, and click **Remove**.
3. To bring it back, add a connection to the same server and share again, click **Test**, then
   **Save**.
4. Answer **Re-attach** when Kaveo asks whether it is the server you removed.

## Good to know

- **Re-attach** also puts its folders back in their categories. **Start fresh** adds it as a new
  connection.
- Its password is deleted from your computer's password store; nothing on the server is touched.
