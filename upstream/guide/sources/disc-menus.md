## What it is for

**Play the disc** plays a Blu-ray or DVD image as a player would: its opening, its own menus, then
the titles you pick from them.

## How to use it

1. Choose **Play the disc** when Kaveo asks, or from the film's page **⋯** menu.
2. **Skip to the menu** jumps the opening.
3. In the menu, move with the arrows and press **Enter**, or point at a button and click it.
4. A menu that plays by itself goes on to the film, as a player's would.

## Good to know

- Over a menu, seeking waits until a title plays; the menu's keys are under **Disc menu** in
  shortcuts.
- The film watched from the menu resumes like the film opened directly; a menu or another title
  keeps no place.
- Java menus (most recent Blu-rays) need Java 8 to 23; without it the film plays and **Discs** says
  why. On Linux, **Install Java for disc menus** there adds one, after asking.
