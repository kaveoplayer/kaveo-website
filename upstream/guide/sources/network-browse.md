## What it is for

Play a file from a NAS or a shared folder without adding it to your library, or find the folder to
add by looking through the share.

## How to use it

1. Click **Sources**, then **Browse** on a network folder's card.
2. Open folders with a click, or with **Tab** and **Enter**; **Up one folder** or **Backspace** goes back.
3. Click a file marked **Play** to watch it. It is not added to your library.
4. To keep a folder, open it and click **Add this folder…**, pick a category, and click **Add folder**.

## Good to know

- A file played from here appears in **Recently played** and resumes where you stopped; one Kaveo
  can't play is listed with the reason, such as **Not a file Kaveo plays**.
- **Browse** opens on the folder you were in last; **Close** or **Esc** ends the browsing.
- A [network address](network-address.md) that names a folder, or a file Kaveo can't play, opens
  the browser there.
