## What it is for

Some films sit on a server as a copy of the whole disc, a Blu-ray disc image, rather than as one
video file. Kaveo plays the film inside it.

## How to use it

1. Open the film's page and click **Play**, as for any other title.
2. The film starts at once, with every soundtrack and subtitle the disc carries; seeking works as
   in any film.

## Good to know

- Kaveo plays the film only: the disc's own menus and extras are not shown yet.
- When the disc holds the film more than once, for example one version per country, Kaveo picks
  the one with the soundtrack language you prefer in the player settings.
- A disc image Kaveo cannot read, an encrypted disc, or a DVD image does not open: Kaveo says why.
