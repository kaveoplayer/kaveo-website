## What it is for

Some films are kept as a copy of the whole disc: a Blu-ray disc image (an ISO file) or a folder
copied from the disc. Kaveo plays the film inside it, from your computer or your Jellyfin server.

## How to use it

1. Put the disc image or the copied disc folder in a library folder. Kaveo lists it as a film.
2. Open the film's page and click **Play**, as for any other title.
3. A disc with its own menu starts as the disc does: its opening, then its menu over the picture.
   Move with the arrows and **Enter**, or click a button. **Skip to the menu** jumps the opening.
4. Any other disc starts its film straight away, with every soundtrack and subtitle it carries.

## Good to know

- While the disc's menu is on screen, the arrows and **Enter** are the menu's, and seeking waits
  until a title plays; the keys can be changed under **Disc menu** in the shortcuts.
- A disc whose menu needs Java, or a film you resume, plays the film itself. A film started
  from the menu keeps its place like any other. A disc Kaveo cannot
  read, an encrypted disc or a DVD image does not open, and Kaveo says why.
- Kaveo picks the film's version with your preferred soundtrack language, and names the forced
  and SDH subtitles once it has read them. A disc folder on Jellyfin plays one soundtrack, no
  subtitles yet.
