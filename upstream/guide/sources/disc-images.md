## What it is for

Some films are kept as a copy of the whole disc: a Blu-ray disc image (an ISO file) or a folder
copied from the disc. Kaveo plays the film inside it, from your computer or your Jellyfin server.

## How to use it

1. Put the disc image or the copied disc folder in a library folder. Kaveo lists it as a film.
2. Open the film's page and click **Play**.
3. For a disc with its own menu, Kaveo asks: **Play the film**, or **Play the disc** from its
   opening, then its menu. Tick **Remember my choice** to stop the question; **Discs** under
   [Behaviour](../settings/behaviour.md) changes it. The page's **⋯** menu offers the other way.
4. In the menu, move with the arrows and **Enter**, or click a button. **Skip to the menu** jumps
   the opening.

## Good to know

- While the menu is on screen, seeking waits until a title plays; its keys can be changed under
  **Disc menu** in the shortcuts.
- A disc whose menu needs Java, a film you resume, and the next title in a list play the film.
  A film started from the menu keeps its place. A disc Kaveo cannot read, an encrypted disc or a
  DVD image does not open, and Kaveo says why.
- Kaveo picks the film's version with your preferred soundtrack language, and names the forced
  and SDH subtitles once it has read them. A disc folder on Jellyfin plays one soundtrack, no
  subtitles yet.
