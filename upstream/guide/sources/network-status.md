## What it is for

Each network connection says whether Kaveo can reach it, so a server that is off or unplugged is
named rather than leaving titles that do not play.

## How to use it

1. Click **Sources**: each connection's card shows **Online**, **Unreachable** or **Sign-in
   refused**, with the reason on hover.
2. Click **Test** on the card to ask the server now.
3. Once the server is back, click **Retry** on the card to read its folders again.

## Good to know

- A category whose network folder can't be reached says so at the top of its page, with **Retry**.
  The line leaves as soon as the folder answers.
- Its titles stay in the library, marked *last seen*, until a scan reads the folder again.
- Above the library, each connection has its own source button beside **Local**, to show only its
  titles.
- A queued [subtitle translation](../subtitles/translation-queue.md) of one of its episodes waits,
  and says why in the queue, while the folder can't be reached, its connection is metered, or a
  title from it is open in the player.
