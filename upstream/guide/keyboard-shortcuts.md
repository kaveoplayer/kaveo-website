## What it is for

These work while a video is playing, and every one of them can be changed — see
[Shortcuts](settings/shortcuts.md), which lists one fewer on Linux.

Six more keys cannot be changed:

- **Esc** leaves fullscreen, on any screen; otherwise it goes back — in the player to where the film was started from, on a library page one step up its path.
- **Ctrl+O** opens **Open file…**, and **Ctrl+L** opens **Open URL…**, on any screen.
- **/** puts the cursor in the search field.
- **F12** saves a picture of what is on screen — see [Screenshots](watching/screenshots.md).
- **Ctrl+H** opens the live picture panel while a film plays.

## Good to know

- The four actions with a dash have no shortcut until you give them one.
- A shortcut can be a mouse button or a wheel notch as readily as a key, so some rows carry both.
- The two delays are also in the player's right-click menu, under **Audio** › **Audio delay** and **Subtitles** › **Timing**.
- **Toggle exclusive fullscreen (Mode 1)** has no Settings row outside Windows, where **Shift+F** is ordinary fullscreen, like **F**.
- Photos have keys of their own — see [Viewing photos](library/view-photos.md).
- While the subtitle editor is open it takes **[**, **]**, **↑** and **↓**: retime the line and move between lines.
- While the video editor (*Edit a copy*) is open it takes **I** and **O**: mark where the copy starts and ends.
- The five **Disc menu** keys work only while a Blu-ray's own menu is on screen, and while it is they take the arrows and **Enter** from the volume, seeking and fullscreen — see [Blu-ray and DVD disc images](sources/disc-images.md).
- **Skip effects for now** compares the sound with and without your Effects, at the same loudness. It lasts until Kaveo closes and is never saved.
