Kaveo is a media player with a built-in library: it plays your local files and can connect to servers
like Jellyfin, but it also works as a plain, fast player — open a file and it plays. This guide is
organised by what you are trying to do.

- [Getting started](getting-started/index.md) — your first minutes with Kaveo.
- [Your library](library/index.md) — browse, organise and correct the titles Kaveo finds.
- [Watching](watching/index.md) — the player, next episode, skipping, screenshots and HDR output.
- [Subtitles](subtitles/index.md) — load, sync, edit and translate them.
- [Sources](sources/index.md) — where your titles come from.
- [Organizing files](organizing/index.md) — naming, imports, and several copies of a title.
- [Private titles](private/index.md) — keep titles encrypted and out of the library until you unlock
  them.
- [From your file manager](file-manager.md) — play or file a video with a right-click.
- [Command line](command-line.md) — start Kaveo on a file or a folder from a terminal.
- [Keyboard shortcuts](keyboard-shortcuts.md) — every key and mouse button the player answers to.
- [Settings](settings/index.md) — a reference page for each section of the settings screen.
- [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md) — the messages Kaveo shows, and what to send when you report a problem.
- [Credits](credits.md) — the people and projects whose work appears in these pages.
