---
title: Private titles
description: Keep titles encrypted and out of the library until you unlock them.
order: 70
---

Kaveo can keep some titles in a **vault**: encrypted on disk, and, while it is locked, not in your
library at all.

- [Setting up private titles](set-up.md) — choose where they live and set a password.
- [Hiding a title](hide-a-title.md) — move a title into the vault.
- [Hiding a photo](hide-a-photo.md) — move one of your own photos into the vault, its Live motion
  with it.
- [Hiding a folder](hide-a-folder.md) — move a whole folder of your own photos and videos into the
  vault.
- [Taking a title back out](take-a-title-out.md) — turn a private title back into an ordinary file.
- [Locking and unlocking](lock.md) — unlock private titles, lock them again, and change the
  password.
