## What it is for

Make a local title private — encrypted where it sits, and out of your library while the vault
is locked.

![The file half of a card's menu: Open containing folder, Edit a copy…, Rename / normalize…, Make private…, Exclude from library…, and under a separator Delete file from disk…](../images/private/hide-a-title-menu.webp)

## How to use it

1. [Unlock the vault](lock.md), then right-click the card of a local film or episode and choose
   **Make private…**.
2. Check the path, then click **Make private**.
3. For a file that is not in your library, open the padlock menu and choose
   **Make a file private…**.
4. Drop a video into your private folder (set one under **Private folder** in
   **Settings › Library › Folders**) and Kaveo hides it once the copy finishes; while the vault is
   locked, the files wait.

![The confirmation asking to move the title into the vault: the film's full path, what will happen to it, and Cancel beside Make private](../images/private/hide-a-title-confirm.webp)

## Good to know

- On the same drive the film is moved, not copied. Encrypting needs its own size again in free
  space, or about 16 MB to encrypt it where it lies.
- The title goes in whole and comes back whole: subtitles, NFO, *-trailer* file and pictures
  named like it, its poster, description and cast, your tags, **My list**, **Favorite** and
  playlists. What a visible title shares stays. A file that cannot go in stays put; the message
  counts them.
- A film's [saved frame](../watching/save-a-frame.md) stays in Pictures, under the film's name, once
  the film is hidden.
- If Kaveo closes during a hide, your next unlock finishes it or puts the film back, and says so.
- You can take it back out, byte for byte, with a valid [licence](../getting-started/licence.md) —
  see [Taking a title back out](take-a-title-out.md).
