## What it is for

Move every photo and video of one of your own folders into the vault at once — each encrypted, and
out of your library while the vault is locked.

## How to use it

1. Right-click the folder's card in your personal category and choose **Move folder to vault…**, or
   open the folder and click **Move to vault…**. Kaveo [creates](set-up.md) or unlocks the vault
   first if needed.
2. Check how many files and how much space, then click **Move to vault**.
3. The files go in one at a time; the padlock shows the one in progress.

## Good to know

- Each file goes in exactly as a single [photo](hide-a-photo.md) or [video](hide-a-title.md) does,
  with its pictures and its Live motion.
- If Kaveo closes before the last one, the rest stay where they are, unencrypted. Open the folder
  again and choose **Move to vault…** for what is left.
- A file playing at that moment is skipped. If one cannot go in, the others stop and the message
  says why.
- The folder itself stays on disk, empty of what went in.
