## What it is for

Undo hiding: the film is decrypted back to a normal file, checked byte for byte against what went in,
and stops being private.

## How to use it

1. [Unlock the vault](lock.md) — a hidden title has no card until you do — then right-click its card
   and choose **Take out of the vault…**.
2. Check the folder the dialog shows — it is where the file came from — or click **Change folder…**
   to put it somewhere else.
3. Click **Take out**. The top bar shows how far it has got, and a close button beside it stops it.

## Good to know

- Taking a title out needs a valid [licence](../getting-started/licence.md).
- Nothing leaves the vault until the check passes: stopping it, or a failure, leaves the title
  private and intact.
- Kaveo never writes over a file, never uses your private folder — anything left there is made
  private again — nor a folder that would not keep the pictures' dates, as some network shares do.
  Pick another folder.
- The subtitles, the NFO file and the pictures that went in come back out beside the film, and its
  watched mark and your place in it come back with them. A picture keeps its name, or is numbered,
  as in *(2)*, when that name is taken.
- The film and its pictures come back with the dates they had — except a film made private by an
  older version of Kaveo, which is dated the day it comes out.
