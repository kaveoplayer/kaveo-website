## What it is for

Make one of your own photos private — encrypted, and out of your library while the vault is locked.
A Live Photo's motion goes in with it.

![A Live Photo's menu on its folder's page: Open containing folder, Copy image, Copy file, Export a copy…, and Make private… at the bottom](../images/private/hide-a-photo-menu.webp)

## How to use it

1. [Unlock the vault](lock.md), then right-click a photo on its folder's page and choose
   **Make private…**.
2. Check the path, then click **Make private**.
3. Or drop the photo into your private folder: Kaveo hides it, and the video of the same name
   beside it as its motion.

![The confirmation asking to move the photo into the vault, its path, and that its Live motion goes with it](../images/private/hide-a-photo-confirm.webp)

## Good to know

- While the vault is unlocked, the photo stays in its folder at its date, and opens in the viewer
  as before — its motion too. A slideshow leaves it out.
- Its picture is never kept anywhere unencrypted, not even its small preview: Kaveo makes it again
  after each unlock.
- A private photo offers no copies. Take it back out to copy or export it — see
  [Taking a title back out](take-a-title-out.md); it comes back with its motion and its dates.
