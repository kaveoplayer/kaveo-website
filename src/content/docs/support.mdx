## Something does not work

Report it as an issue on GitHub. One problem per issue, please — and search first, in case someone
has already reported it.

<LinkButton href={ISSUES_URL} icon="external" variant="primary">Report a bug</LinkButton>

A report we can act on has three things in it:

1. **What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead** — and any message Kaveo showed,
   which it can copy for you. See [Troubleshooting](guide/troubleshooting.md).
2. **The version you are running**, from **Settings › About**. See [About](guide/settings/about.md).
3. **A diagnostic bundle**: under **Settings › Diagnostics**, raise **Detail level**, do the thing
   that failed again, then click **Export a diagnostic bundle** and attach the zip. See
   [Diagnostics](guide/settings/diagnostics.md).

The bundle holds recent logs, any crash reports and your machine's details. Your library folders,
servers and lists are left out, your home folder and user name are removed, and the zip lists what
it left out — but an issue is public, so look inside before you attach it.

## A question, or an idea

Ask in Discussions. It is also where to suggest a feature.

<LinkButton href={DISCUSSIONS_URL} icon="external" variant="secondary">Open Discussions</LinkButton>

Most "how do I…" questions are answered in the [guide](guide/index.md).

## Licences

Asking for a beta key, a key that will not activate, or anything else about your licence: write to
<a href={`mailto:${LICENCE_EMAIL}`}>{LICENCE_EMAIL}</a>. Never post a licence key in an issue or a
discussion — both are public.
