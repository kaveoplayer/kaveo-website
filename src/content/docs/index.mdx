<Moment num="01" kicker="Your library" title="Your folders, laid out like a streaming service.">

Kaveo finds the films, series and music in your folders and puts them in rows, with posters and
plots from TMDB. Group a saga, see which episodes are missing — and connect a Jellyfin server if you
have one: its titles play beside your own, keeping your place in sync.

Some titles are nobody else's business: keep them in an encrypted vault, and while it is locked they
are not in your library at all.

[Your library →](guide/library/index.md)

<LibraryFacts slot="visual" />
</Moment>

<Moment num="02" kicker="The picture" title="The way it was mastered." flip>

An HDR film goes to an HDR-capable display the way it was mastered; an ordinary video never switches
the display. Each frame goes through a picture pipeline — repaired, enlarged, shrunk, finished —
with a neural model among its choices for lower-resolution sources.

[HDR output →](guide/watching/hdr.md) · [Upscaler →](guide/settings/upscaler.md)

<PlayerFrame slot="visual" />
</Moment>

<Moment num="03" kicker="Subtitles" title="Found, in time, in your language.">

Load a subtitle beside the video or inside it, or find one on OpenSubtitles. Move it onto the right
timing, fix a line, or translate it — a film or a whole season — with an AI model online, or on your
own computer with Ollama or LM Studio.

[Subtitles →](guide/subtitles/index.md)

</Moment>

## A program on your computer.

Your library, your watch history and your settings stay there. No analytics, no usage statistics, no
crash reports. TMDB, OpenSubtitles and Jellyfin are used only once you set them up, and Kaveo talks
to each one directly. [What Kaveo sends, and to whom](privacy.md)
