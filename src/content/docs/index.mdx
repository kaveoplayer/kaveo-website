<h2>Before you start</h2>

<details>
<summary>How do I get a licence key?</summary>

During the beta, write to <a href={`mailto:${LICENCE_EMAIL}`}>{LICENCE_EMAIL}</a>, or click **Get a
key** on Kaveo's licence screen. A key lasts 30 days and arrives by email. See
[Licence](guide/getting-started/licence.md).

</details>

<details>
<summary>What happens when the key runs out?</summary>

A badge and a message say so in its last week. When it ends, Kaveo shows only the licence screen
until a new key is entered — a film you are watching plays to its end first, and nothing is deleted.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Does it need an internet connection?</summary>

Your own files play without one. Kaveo checks for updates (which you can turn off) and confirms the
licence's date at least once a week; everything else goes only to the services you set up. See
[Privacy](privacy.md).

</details>

<details>
<summary>Which systems does it run on?</summary>

Linux (x86_64, with a Vulkan 1.2 GPU) and 64-bit Windows 10 or 11. macOS is not supported. See
[Download](download.mdx).

</details>

</div>
