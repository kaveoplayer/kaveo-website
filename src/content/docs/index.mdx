## What it does

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="A library, built from your folders" icon="list-format">
    Kaveo finds the films, series and music in your folders and lays them out in rows, with posters
    and plots from TMDB once you give it a key. Split it into categories of your own, group a saga, and see which episodes you are
    missing. See [Your library](guide/library/index.md).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Or just play a file" icon="rocket">
    Drag a video onto the window, use **Open file**, start it from a terminal or from your file
    manager's right-click menu — no library needed. See [Getting started](guide/getting-started/index.md).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Jellyfin, if you have it" icon="server">
    Connect a Jellyfin server and its films and series join your library, playing beside your local
    files and keeping your place in sync. See [Jellyfin](guide/sources/jellyfin.md).
  </Card>
  <Card title="HDR output" icon="sun">
    An HDR film goes to an HDR-capable display the way it was mastered; an ordinary video never
    switches the display. See [HDR output](guide/watching/hdr.md).
  </Card>
  <Card title="A neural upscaler" icon="star">
    A picture pipeline that repairs, enlarges and finishes each frame, with a neural model as one of
    its choices for lower-resolution sources. See [Upscaler](guide/settings/upscaler.md).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Subtitles it can fetch, sync and translate" icon="comment-alt">
    Load a subtitle beside the video or inside it, find one on OpenSubtitles, move it onto the right
    timing, edit it, or translate it with an AI model — online or on your own computer. See
    [Subtitles](guide/subtitles/index.md).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Private titles" icon="padlock">
    Keep some titles in an encrypted vault: while it is locked, they are not in your library at all.
    See [Private titles](guide/private/index.md).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Linux and Windows" icon="laptop">
    Packages for Ubuntu, Debian, Fedora and Arch, a tarball and an AppImage; an installer, an MSI and a
    portable build for Windows. See [Download](download.mdx).
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
